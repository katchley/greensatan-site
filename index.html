greensatan is an expansive music project —rooted in improvisation and psychedelia— that includes solo work (greensatan) and collaborations with other artists (greensatan project)—notably Kattt Atchley.
The music betrays influences of noise, minimal techno, post avant-garde electronics, genre (horror) film-scores/sound-design.
